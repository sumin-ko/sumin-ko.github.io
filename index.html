In my M.A. thesis (Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Moosung Lee), I studied teaching practice ambidexterity: how teachers combine exploratory practices, such as open-ended and cognitively demanding tasks, with exploitative practices, such as clarifying learning goals and consolidating prior content. Using OECD TALIS 2024 data and latent profile analysis, I identified four distinct profiles (low-exploitation/low-exploration, exploitation-focused, restricted-practice, and highly ambidextrous) and used multilevel models to test how these profiles relate to teachers’ occupational stress. I am now extending this work to ask whether teacher self-efficacy and school-level teacher leadership climate shape that relationship.

Looking ahead, I plan to bring this lens to how teachers respond to new instructional and policy demands, including the integration of AI into classrooms. I am particularly interested in why such demands feel manageable in some organizational contexts and overwhelming in others, and how school- and district-level policies and supports can help teachers implement change without adding unnecessary burden.

I am currently a pre-doctoral trainee at the Institute of Population and Human Capital, Yonsei University, and I am applying to Ph.D. programs in education policy in the U.S. for Fall 2027.

Relationship between Teaching Practice Ambidexterity Types and Teachers’ Occupational Stress수업 실행 양손잡이성 유형과 교사 스트레스 간 관계 분석

Ko, S., & Lee, M. (2026). Korean Journal of Educational Research, 64(6). Based on my M.A. thesis.

고수민, 이무성 (2026). 『교육학연구』, 64(6). 석사학위논문을 수정·보완한 연구.

DOI coming soonDOI 추가 예정
Abstract

This study examined how exploratory and exploitative teaching practices co-occur at different levels and in different configurations among teachers, conceptualizing these patterns as “Teaching Practice Ambidexterity.” Latent profile analysis using data from Korean teachers and principals in TALIS 2024 identified four types of teaching practice ambidexterity: low-exploitation/low-exploration, exploitation-focused, restricted-practice, and highly ambidextrous. Teachers’ background and task-related characteristics significantly predicted the probability of latent class membership, whereas schools’ contextual characteristics showed no significant associations. The highly ambidextrous type showed generally higher levels of self-efficacy, teacher leadership, teacher autonomy, and digital device use. Meanwhile, teaching practice ambidexterity type showed no significant association with either task-related stress (instruction, assessment, and administrative work) or student-related stress. However, teacher leadership was negatively associated with task-related stress, and teacher self-efficacy was negatively associated with student-related stress. These findings indicate that teaching practices are qualitatively differentiated by how exploration and exploitation are combined. Implications of these findings for research, practice, and policy are discussed.

Keywords: Teaching Practice Ambidexterity, Exploratory Teaching Practice, Exploitative Teaching Practice, Teachers’ Occupational Stress, TALIS

요약

본 연구는 교사의 탐색적 수업 실행과 활용적 수업 실행이 어떠한 수준과 조합으로 함께 나타나는지에 주목하여, 이를 ‘수업 실행 양손잡이성(Teaching Practice Ambidexterity)’으로 개념화하였다. TALIS 2024 한국 중학교 교사 및 교장 자료를 활용하여 잠재프로파일분석을 진행한 결과, 수업 실행 양손잡이성은 저활용·저탐색형, 활용 집중형, 제한적 실행형, 고수준 양손잡이형의 네 유형으로 구분되었다. 교사의 배경적·과업적 특성은 잠재집단 소속 가능성을 유의하게 예측한 반면, 학교의 구조적 특성은 유의한 관련을 보이지 않았다. 심리적·전문적 특성에서도 유형 간 차이가 확인되었으며, 특히 탐색과 활용을 모두 높은 수준으로 수행하는 고수준 양손잡이형은 자기효능감, 교사 리더십, 교사 자율성 및 디지털 기기 활용 등에서 전반적으로 높은 수준으로 나타났다. 한편, 수업 실행 양손잡이성 유형은 수업·평가·행정 과업 스트레스와 학생지도 스트레스 모두와 유의한 관련을 보이지 않았다. 그러나, 교사 리더십은 과업 스트레스와, 교사 자기효능감은 학생지도 스트레스와 각각 부적인 관련을 나타냈다. 이러한 결과는 교사의 수업 실행이 탐색과 활용이 결합되는 방식에 따라 질적으로 구분된다는 것을 보여준다. 이를 바탕으로 수업 실행 양손잡이성을 교사의 수업 실행을 이해하기 위한 하나의 유형으로 제시하고, 탐색적 수업 실행과 활용적 수업 실행의 결합 양상을 고려한 지원 방안을 모색할 필요가 있음을 시사한다.

주제어: 수업 실행 양손잡이성, 탐색적 수업 실행, 활용적 수업 실행, 교사 스트레스, TALIS

Schools face advances in digital technology, growing learner diversity, and calls for new ways of teaching. Teachers are asked to maintain established practices while exploring new instructional strategies.

Organization research has examined this tension for decades. In education, research on how individual teachers enact it, and how it relates to their psychological well-being, has been limited.

교육 현장은 디지털 기술의 발전, 학습자 다양성의 확대, 수업 방식의 변화 요구 속에 있습니다. 교사는 기존의 수업 방식을 유지하는 동시에 새로운 수업 전략을 탐색해야 하는 양가적 요구에 직면합니다.

조직 연구는 이 긴장을 오랫동안 다뤄 왔지만, 교사 개인이 이를 어떻게 실행하는지, 그리고 그것이 교사의 심리적 웰빙과 어떻게 관련되는지를 다룬 교육학 연구는 아직 제한적입니다.

Exploratory practice탐색적 수업 실행

Open-ended, cognitively demanding tasks; students search for alternative solutions.

개방형·고차원적 사고 과제, 학생의 대안 탐색

Exploitative practice활용적 수업 실행

Clarifying learning goals, consolidating prior content, structured delivery.

학습목표 명료화, 선행 내용 정리, 구조화된 내용 전달

Teaching practice ambidexterity captures how both combine within one teacher’s classroom, adapting March’s (1991) exploration–exploitation distinction to teaching.

수업 실행 양손잡이성은 두 실행이 한 교사의 수업 안에서 결합되는 양상을 뜻합니다. March(1991)의 탐색–활용 구분을 수업 맥락에 적용한 개념입니다.

A firm can assign exploration and exploitation to separate units. A teacher cannot. Teaching is a case of contextual ambidexterity: one person integrates competing demands within limited time and space.

This integration can expand professional expertise, and it can also bring role conflict and cognitive overload. The link to stress is interpreted through the Job Demands–Resources model: job demands may not lead to stress when sufficient resources offset them.

기업은 탐색과 활용을 서로 다른 부서에 나눠 맡길 수 있지만 교사는 그럴 수 없습니다. 수업은 한 사람이 제한된 시간과 공간 안에서 상충하는 요구를 스스로 통합하는 맥락적 양손잡이성의 성격을 띱니다.

이 통합은 전문성을 넓히는 계기가 될 수 있고, 동시에 역할 갈등과 인지적 과부하를 낳을 수도 있습니다. 스트레스와의 관계는 직무요구-자원(JD-R) 모형으로 해석했습니다. 직무 요구는 이를 상쇄할 자원이 충분할 때 스트레스로 이어지지 않을 수 있습니다.

The Impact of Multicultural Youth Policy Participation during High School on Academic Persistence Intentions and College Adaptation고등학교 시기의 다문화청소년지원정책 참여가 학업지속의향과 대학생활적응에 미치는 영향

Ko, S., & Lee, A. (2025). Multicultural Education Studies, 18(2), 61–88. First and corresponding author.

고수민, 이아란 (2025). 『다문화교육연구』, 18(2), 61–88. 제1저자·교신저자.

Read the article논문 보기
Abstract

This study examines the impact of multicultural youth policy participation during high school on academic persistence intentions and college adaptation among multicultural youth in late adolescence, using data from the Multicultural Adolescents Panel Study (MAPS). To ensure internal validity, the analysis was conducted in three stages: a basic regression model, a model controlling for background variables, and a propensity score matching (PSM) approach. Findings indicate that policy participation significantly enhances academic persistence intentions. Students who did not participate in the policy showed lower levels of persistence intentions, suggesting a higher risk of academic dropout. However, no significant relationship was found between policy participation and either academic or social aspects of college adaptation, implying that individual, social, and familial factors play a more substantial role in shaping college adaptation outcomes. By extending the scope of analysis into late adolescence, this study highlights the need for continuous, developmentally appropriate support that reflects the evolving needs of multicultural youth and fosters their sustained engagement in higher education.

Keywords: multicultural youth, multicultural youth policy, academic persistence intention, college adaptation, MAPS

When Is Teaching Practice Ambidexterity Professionally Rewarding? The Moderating Role of School Leadership

Ko, S. (2026, November). 3rd International Conference of Global Studies in Education, The Education University of Hong Kong.

Abstract

Recent educational reforms emphasizing student-centered instruction require teachers to combine structured instruction with exploratory approaches. Drawing on the concept of ambidexterity, this study conceptualizes practices that organize and deliver structured content as exploitative teaching practices, and practices that promote students’ critical thinking and active engagement as exploratory teaching practices. Teaching practice ambidexterity refers to teachers’ coordination of potentially competing forms of teaching practice.

Using TALIS 2024 Shanghai data, this study identified four teaching practice profiles through latent profile analysis: Limited, Bounded Balanced, Exploitative-oriented, and High Ambidextrous. The study then estimated multilevel models to examine whether these profiles were associated with teachers’ job satisfaction with the profession and whether school leadership strengthened this association. Because instructional and relational leadership are closely related but conceptually distinct, they were modeled separately.

The results show that High Ambidextrous teachers reported higher job satisfaction than those in the Bounded Balanced reference profile when school leadership was at the Shanghai average level. This pattern was marginal in the instructional leadership models and significant in the relational leadership models. Both forms of school leadership amplified this association: the interactions with the High Ambidextrous profile were positive and significant for both leadership models. Simple slope tests showed that school leadership had the strongest positive association with job satisfaction among High Ambidextrous teachers, for both instructional leadership and relational leadership.

These findings suggest that teaching practice ambidexterity can be professionally rewarding when supported by school leadership. For school leaders, the results highlight the importance of providing instructional direction and relational support so that teachers can turn complex teaching demands into positive professional experiences.
